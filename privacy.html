ARORA Games Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy covers the iPhone games Marble Belt: Color Sort, Skyline Slice: Stack Tower and Knotwick: Rope Untangle Puzzle (the "Games"), published by ARORA Games.

Your progress on this device

Your level, best score, coins and sound settings are saved only on your iPhone. Deleting a game deletes that progress. There is no account and no sign-up in the current version.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games show ads from Google AdMob so they can stay free. You will see a full-screen ad between some later levels, and you can choose to watch an optional ad for a bonus (for example an extra continue or double coins). The first two levels, and the first couple of minutes of a session, do not show automatic ads.

To serve and measure these ads, Google and its partners may collect:

  • a device identifier (including the iOS advertising identifier, or IDFA, if you allow tracking)
  • information about ads you have seen and how you interact with them
  • coarse location, which can be derived from your IP address (the Games do not ask for your precise location)
  • diagnostic information such as crash logs and performance data
  • basic app-interaction information, such as launching the game or watching a video ad

Google may use this information for advertising (including personalized advertising when you allow it), advertising measurement and analytics. Google's own policy is at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices (ATT and consent)

On iPhone, Apple will ask whether you want to allow the Games to track you across other companies' apps and websites. That is the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalized.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or certain US states, you will also see a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before ads are requested. You can change those choices later from your device settings, or, where required, from a privacy-options link once we add one in a later update.

Children

The Games are rated for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not tag the Games as child-directed for COPPA, and we do not enroll them in Apple's Kids category. Personalized ads are shown only after you allow tracking; otherwise ads are non-personalized.

Accounts (not in this version)

A later update may add an optional sign-in (Sign in with Apple, Google or email) to back up your progress. Sign-in will stay optional, and you will be able to delete your account inside the Games. We will update this policy before that feature goes live.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will update this page and the date above.
