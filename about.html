About

A note from Devin, the person behind these games.

← ARORA Games

Hi, I'm Devin.

I created ARORA Games because I wanted to make fun, casual games for people who just need something to kill time with, something to distract themselves, or something they simply enjoy playing. A few minutes on the couch, a puzzle on a break — that's the kind of play I had in mind.

This is my first run at creating anything like this, and I'm just hoping I can create something that people enjoy playing.

Marble Belt, Skyline Slice, and Knotwick are original casual puzzles for iPhone. They're coming soon to the App Store, free to play with light ads, and ready to play offline.

I welcome all feedback, any improvements that could be made, and any features you'd like to see in any of the games. If you have an idea, or if something could work better, I'd love to hear from you.

The games

Three original casual puzzles, each with its own look, music and one-thumb controls.

  • Marble Belt: Color Sort Tap & match conveyor puzzle
  • Skyline Slice: Stack Tower One-tap city building arcade
  • Knotwick: Rope Untangle Puzzle Tap the arrows, free the ropes

Your feedback is welcome

Please write to me at devin.hardy.business@gmail.com. Tell me what you enjoyed, what could be improved, or what you'd like to see next in any of the games.

If you need help with one of the games, the Support page is a good place to start.
