Marble Belt: Color Sort

Tap & match conveyor puzzle

Marbles ride around a moving conveyor belt. Tap a marble that matches an open cup and it drops right in. New colors drop in as you play, and later on, shifter marbles swap color for a score multiplier — more of them, and faster, as you climb. Fill every cup before time runs out. Sounds easy, until the belt gets busy.

  1. 1Tap a marble on the belt to send it to the cup of the same color.
  2. 2Wrong taps go to the tray, so choose carefully.
  3. 3Fill every cup before the timer runs out.
  • One-thumb tap controls and short, quick levels
  • Chain matches for combos and bigger scores
  • New twists as you climb: arriving colors, shifter marbles that get faster and more frequent, color order, locked cups, stones, rainbows and bombs
  • Bright neon look, chunky sound effects and its own music track
  • Play offline

Free to play and supported by ads. Optional ads can double your coins or add a tray slot. The first two levels stay ad-free, and you always get one free continue per level.

Casual · Arcade

Skyline Slice: Stack Tower

One-tap city building arcade

Build skyscrapers one floor at a time. Each new floor slides across the sunset. Tap to drop it onto your tower. Whatever hangs over the edge gets sliced off — land a perfect drop to keep the full width, and chain three perfects to grow it back.

  1. 1Tap anywhere to drop the sliding floor.
  2. 2Overhang gets sliced off, so line it up.
  3. 3Reach the target height to finish the building.
  • One-tap timing you can learn in seconds
  • Perfect-drop combos with rising chimes
  • Every finished building joins the city skyline behind you
  • Taller towers, faster floors and a slow-mo booster as you level up
  • Warm sunset art and its own music track · Play offline

Free to play and supported by ads. Optional ads can rewind a missed floor or double your gems. The first two buildings stay ad-free, and you always get one free rewind per building.

Puzzle · Strategy

Knotwick: Rope Untangle Puzzle

Tap the arrows, free the ropes

A cozy logic puzzle set on a craft table. Every rope has an arrow on its end. Tap a rope and it slides out the way the arrow points, but only if nothing is in its path. Later, numbered ropes can be pinned — pull the marked key rope first to unlock 1, then 2, then 3. No timers, just thinking.

  1. 1Look at where each arrow points.
  2. 2Tap a rope with a clear path to pull it off the board.
  3. 3Clear every rope before you run out of hearts.
  • One-tap puzzles with no timers
  • Numbered, pinned, locked and decoy ropes as the tangles grow
  • Every board is built to be solvable
  • Hints when you are stuck, paid with yarn you earn
  • Hand-drawn paper-craft look, soft plucky sounds and its own music track · Play offline

Free to play and supported by ads. Optional ads can grant extra yarn or another try. The first two puzzles stay ad-free, and you always get one free continue per puzzle.
