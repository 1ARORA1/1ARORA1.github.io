ARORA Games Support

Help for Marble Belt, Skyline Slice and Knotwick.

Contact us

Email devin.hardy.business@gmail.com with the game name, your iPhone model and what happened. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.

How do I turn off music or sound? Use the ♫ Sound and Music buttons on the home screen or the pause menu.

Why am I seeing ads? The games are free and supported by ads. Automatic ads appear only after the first two levels and a short wait, between levels. Optional “Watch ad” bonuses are never required to keep playing. You always get one free continue per level.

I'm stuck on a level. Each game has a booster you can buy with coins you earn (an extra tray slot, slow-mo or a hint). Marble Belt also offers a free shuffle after a few tries.
